Cost: about $6.15 on the Meta API by the local counter (three runs, resumed after Overpass timeouts), which overstates the real bill; answers are cached, so a rerun is free.

Limits: no human labels for the water, so these are SAM's outlines, not measured flood extent; permanent water and wet fields are found too (a truck should avoid them, but they are not the flood); water under trees is missed, so shares are lower bounds there; OSM streets are incomplete in some towns; one square per place, chosen by the author and centred on the town, not on the streets known to have flooded, so most squares hold little water and the pooled share says how common flooding was in a town-centre square, not how bad it was where it hit; three Harvey squares taken in poor light (beaumont, friendswood, houston-kingwood: 0% water, 3 roofs found in the last) are probably under-read by SAM; photos of one town were taken on different days of each event; 'under water' means the street lies inside a water outline, not that it is impassable to every vehicle.
