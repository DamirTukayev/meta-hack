# Second look: two models, the same crops

Generated by `scripts/eval_looks.py`. On 44 photos the 3 longest stretches of mapped road that the SAM mask does not confirm were cropped with the stretch marked (as the product does) and shown to Muse Spark 1.3 (Meta API) and Muse Spark Open (Baseten): 130 stretches.
There are no labels: this measures how often the two agree and what they say, not which is right. Only standing water or debris would make the agent plan around a stretch; the other answers leave the plan as it is.

| What they said | Muse Spark 1.3 | Muse Spark Open |
|---|---|---|
| clear_road | 43 | 41 |
| hidden_by_trees_or_shadow | 18 | 18 |
| no_road_visible | 66 | 68 |
| standing_water | 3 | 3 |

Same answer: **92 of 130** (71%). Same consequence for the plan (blocked or not): **100 of 130** (77%). Where they differed (Muse Spark 1.3 → Muse Spark Open): clear_road → no_road_visible ×14, no_road_visible → clear_road ×12, no_road_visible → hidden_by_trees_or_shadow ×4, hidden_by_trees_or_shadow → no_road_visible ×4, hidden_by_trees_or_shadow → clear_road ×2, clear_road → hidden_by_trees_or_shadow ×2.
Cost of this run: about $0.62 on the Meta API and $1.33 on Baseten. Limits: no ground truth, the choice of stretches (the longest unconfirmed ones) favours trees and shadow, and the crops are one 1.5 km tile each.
