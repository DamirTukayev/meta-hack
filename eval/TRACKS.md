# Dirt tracks: the map graph against the graph read from the photo

Generated by `scripts/eval_tracks.py`. Three 1.5 km forest squares whose OpenStreetMap roads (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL) are mostly `highway=track`. SAM 3.1 reads the road mask of each
photo; two graphs are built, one from the mask and one from the map. "Reached" = the mapped track lies within 15 m of the graph read from the photo; "followed" = within 8 m of the road mask.

| Apalachicola NF, FL | 4.3 km | 4.3 km | 3.1 km | 69% | 74% | 21% / 28% | 4 / 5 |
| Sam Houston NF, TX | 4.8 km | 3.9 km | 0.0 km | 0% | 0% | 0% / 32% | 0 / 4 |

Muse Spark was shown the longest stretches of track the photo does not confirm (9 stretches, a crop with the stretch marked) and said: hidden_by_trees_or_shadow 6, clear_road 2, no_road_visible 1.
Cost of this run: about $1.35 (SAM, read once and cached). Limits: three squares picked because the map has tracks there; OSM tracks are mapped by people and some may be overgrown or gone; the random scenarios are not real requests; the model's answers were checked by eye on the saved crops, not against labels.
