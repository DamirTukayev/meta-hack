# Second look on flooded towns: two models and SAM's water

Generated by `scripts/eval_looks_flood.py`. On 37 NOAA squares of flooded towns (the squares of `eval/FLOOD_SWEEP.md`) the 5 longest stretches of mapped road that the SAM mask does not confirm were cropped with the stretch marked and shown to Muse Spark 1.3 (Meta API) and Muse Spark Open (Baseten): 175 stretches. For each stretch the share of its length inside the water outlines SAM 3.1 found on the whole photo was measured as a second, independent route to the same fact.
There are no human labels, and both routes are models: this measures how often independent perception agrees, not which is right.

| What they said | Muse Spark 1.3 | Muse Spark Open |
|---|---|---|
| clear_road | 43 | 28 |
| debris | 0 | 2 |
| hidden_by_trees_or_shadow | 64 | 53 |
| no_road_visible | 49 | 69 |
| standing_water | 19 | 23 |

The two models gave the same answer on **130 of 175** (74%) and the same consequence for the plan (blocked or not) on **146** (83%).

| Stretches | How many | Muse Spark 1.3 said blocked (water or debris) | Muse Spark Open said blocked |
|---|---|---|---|
| at least half inside SAM's water | 11 | 10 of 11 (91%) | 11 of 11 (100%) |
| under a tenth inside SAM's water | 160 | 6 of 160 (4%) | 11 of 160 (7%) |

Cost of this run: about $13.71 on the Meta API and $1.27 on Baseten by the local counters, which overstate the real bills. Limits: no ground truth; the stretches are the longest ones the road mask does not confirm, which favours trees and shadow; SAM's water is itself a model output and misses water under trees; a crop is one stretch of one 1.5 km square; the product also forbids every road inside SAM's water before the second look, so this tests the look on stretches that are often already avoided.
